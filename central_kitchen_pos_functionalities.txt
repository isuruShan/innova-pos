INNOVA-POS: CENTRAL KITCHEN ENABLED FUNCTIONALITIES (STORE LEVEL)

This document outlines the core functional requirements and workflows for store-level
POS instances when the Central Kitchen/Commissary model is enabled.

================================================================================
1. STORE-LEVEL STOCKS
================================================================================
* Store-Specific Relevance: Store-level inventory lists are strictly local and
  only display stocks relevant to the specific store.
* Origin Restriction: The stock levels displayed will ONLY reflect inventory items
  that have been transferred from the Central Kitchen. Items that have never been
  sent/received from the Central Kitchen will not appear or will show a zero count.

================================================================================
2. MANUAL STOCK ADJUSTMENTS
================================================================================
* Restrictive Adjustment: Manual adjustments are only allowed on items that were
  transferred to the store from the Central Kitchen.
* Role-Based Access Control: This functionality is restricted and only available
  to users with the Manager or Inventory Clerk roles. Regular cashiers or other
  staff cannot perform adjustments.

================================================================================
3. COUNT SHEETS & STOCKTAKE AUDITS
================================================================================
* Restrictive Auditing: Count sheets and stocktake sessions can only contain and audit
  inventory items that were transferred from the Central Kitchen.
* Role-Based Access Control: Configuring count sheets and executing/committing
  stocktake audits is restricted to Manager and Inventory Clerk roles only.

================================================================================
4. INTER-STORE STOCK TRANSFERS WORKFLOW (COMPREHENSIVE WORKFLOW)
================================================================================
* A comprehensive, multi-step workflow governs stock transfers between the
  Central Kitchen and the retail stores.

  Step 1: Store-Level Request
  - The retail store initiates a "Transfer Request" to order specific inventory
    quantities from the Central Kitchen.

  Step 2: Status Tracking
  - Both parties can track the request state in real-time through standard statuses
    (e.g., Requested -> Approved -> Shipped/In-Transit -> Received/Completed).

  Step 3: Dispatch (Central Kitchen)
  - The Central Kitchen reviews the request, prepares/packages the items, and
    dispatches/ships them. The items are marked "In-Transit".

  Step 4: Mismatch Auditing (Store Level)
  - Upon delivery, the store inspects the shipment and matches the actual items
    received against the original transfer request quantities to check for shortages
    or overages.

  Step 5: Transfer GRN (Goods Receipt Note) Completion
  - The store completes a specialized GRN for that transfer request, committing
    the verified quantities.
  - CRITICAL RULE: Store stock count levels are ONLY updated once this Transfer GRN
    is completed and saved. Until then, items remain in-transit and store stock counts
    remain unchanged.

================================================================================
5. WASTAGE MANAGEMENT
================================================================================
* Applicable Items: Wastage logging (spillage, damage, expiry) is only applicable
  for items that have been transferred to the store from the Central Kitchen.
* No raw/untransferred kitchen items or unrelated products can be logged for wastage
  at the store level.

================================================================================
6. MODULE STATUSES (ENABLED / DISABLED)
================================================================================
* Supplier Management: DISABLED. Stores do not manage supplier profiles.
* Purchasing & Purchase Orders (POs): DISABLED. Stores do not issue POs directly
  to external suppliers (all replenishment happens via Transfer Requests).
* Goods Receipts & Returns (GRN): DISABLED. General external GRN is disabled.
  (Only the specialized GRN for Inter-Store Transfer Requests is enabled).
* Live Stock Movements Log (Audit Trail): ENABLED. Tracks all local stock actions
  (Transfers In, Manual Adjustments, Audits, Wastage) with timestamps, quantities,
  previous/new counts, and the user responsible.
